1. **Meta sinks nearly 7% on negative free cash flow from AI spend, dragging Nasdaq down 2.6% and 10-year Treasury yield below 5%**  
   Reversing the initial positive reception to its third-quarter beat and Muse AI roadmap, Meta Platforms shares sank nearly 7% after the company disclosed further AI spending commitments that pushed its free cash flow negative, drove its five-year credit-default swaps to all-time highs, and accompanied plans to create a new class of non-voting shares. The renewed alarm over hyperscaler cash burn and AI capital needs dragged the Nasdaq down 2.6% and sparked a flight to safety that sent the 10-year U.S. Treasury yield below 5% in its biggest drop since 2023.

2. **U.S. evacuates Saudi embassy staff after Houthi strikes and reportedly readies post-midterm Iran strikes as Qatar buys U.S. LNG**  
   Escalating the Middle East conflict, the U.S. ordered non-emergency diplomatic staff out of Saudi Arabia following Houthi strikes on an airport and an oil field, while reportedly preparing strikes against Iran after the U.S. midterm elections. The intensifying hostilities pushed oil futures higher and prompted QatarEnergy—whose own production has been throttled by the Iran war—to sign its first U.S. offtake deal with Cheniere Energy for 4 million tons of liquefied natural gas annually through 2054.

3. **French-German 10-year bond spread hits widest since 2011 as French yields reach new 24-year high**  
   Building on France's 10-year bond yield crossing 5%, the yield climbed to a new 24-year high on Thursday even as U.S. and German yields fell, widening the 10-year French-German spread to its highest level since 2011. The divergence underscores France's fiscal vulnerability ahead of another round of weekend protests, with President Emmanuel Macron condemning external criticism as bond market pressure builds.

4. **Japan September core inflation hits 2.1% to top BOJ target for first time this year**  
   Japan's core inflation rose to 2.1% in September, matching economist forecasts and reaching its highest level this year. The reading pushed the key price gauge above the Bank of Japan's target for the first time in 2026, reinforcing expectations for a December BOJ interest rate hike as the yen also rallied to a one-month high on haven demand.

5. **China stocks sink deeper into bear market despite state-backed fund buying**  
   Chinese equities slid deeper into a bear market following a record losing streak, as stock purchases by state-backed funds failed to restore investor confidence despite a recent rebound in sovereign wealth fund investments into China and Hong Kong.
