# Market Watch — 2026-11-05

Updated: 2026-11-05T07:19:31.419219-05:00

1. **Democrats flip seven Senate seats and nearly 30 House districts as stocks surge, long-end yields hit eight-week lows, and Musk and defense stocks slide**  
   Expanding on the initial U.S. midterm election results, Democrats flipped seven Senate seats—including wins in Texas—nearly 30 House districts, five governorships, and up to 12 state legislative chambers without losing a single seat to Republicans. In the ensuing trading sessions, U.S. equities surged—led by Apple, Amazon, Google, Meta, and Microsoft—while long-end Treasury yields fell to eight-week lows and the U.S. dollar posted its steepest drop in two months amid expectations of legislative gridlock. However, the election outcome triggered sharp sector losses: stocks tied to Elon Musk erased $120 billion in value after the Democratic win in Texas, U.S. and European defense stocks fell on reduced expectations for military spending growth, and data-center plays declined following election backlash.

2. **Brent crude plunges below $100 on hopes Democratic Congress will hasten end to Iran war even as Russia supplies Tehran with missile launchers**  
   Following earlier oil market volatility around U.S.-Iran hostilities and peace talk hopes, Brent crude futures plunged up to 10% to trade below $100 a barrel and held near a two-month low as traders bet the Democratic sweep of Congress will accelerate efforts to end the war in the Middle East. The steep drop in crude prices also supported gains in U.S. Treasuries. Separately, Russia agreed to supply Iran with 90 launchers for advanced guided missiles, deepening its military support for Tehran.

3. **CVS Health shares fall 10% on plan to split off retail drugstore business**  
   CVS Health announced plans to split off its retail drugstore business after a roughly two-year turnaround, effectively dismantling the vertically integrated healthcare conglomerate created by its $78 billion acquisition of Aetna in 2018. Shares of CVS fell 10% following the breakup announcement.

4. **Rheinmetall shares plunge 17% after cutting 2026 sales guidance for a second time on military contract delays**  
   German defense contractor Rheinmetall cut its 2026 sales outlook for the second time in three months, citing delays in German military projects—including the Boxer armored vehicle program—and artillery ammunition deliveries that will push orders into next year. The guidance reduction sent Rheinmetall shares plunging 17%.

5. **Nintendo Switch 2 Q2 sales plunge 41% after price hikes tied to soaring memory costs**  
   Nintendo reported that sales of its flagship Switch 2 console plunged 41% in its fiscal second quarter after the company raised prices in response to soaring memory chip costs.
