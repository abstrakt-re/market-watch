# Market Watch — 2026-10-22

Updated: 2026-10-22T07:22:21.122800-04:00

1. **Meta beats Q3 estimates and outlines Muse AI monetization amid $25B New Mexico penalty**  
   Meta Platforms reported a strong third quarter and guidance while laying out an ambitious monetization roadmap for its Muse AI model, which it launched as Muse Spark Open last week. Separately, the New Mexico Department of Justice ordered Meta to pay a $25 billion penalty in its Cambridge Analytica case.

2. **Tesla beats Q3 expectations despite lower profit and auto revenue as SpaceX earnings lift AI stocks**  
   Building on SpaceX's earnings beat and merger committee announcement, SpaceX shares jumped 8% and joined Super Micro Computer's preliminary fiscal first-quarter revenue guidance raise to $18.5 billion–$19.5 billion (up from $14.5 billion–$15.5 billion) in sparking a relief rally across U.S. chip and AI stocks. Tesla subsequently reported third-quarter earnings and sales that beat Wall Street expectations even as auto revenue declined and profit fell with batteries proving a barrier to growth, while CEO Elon Musk declined further comment on a SpaceX tie-up.

3. **French 10-year bond yield hits 5% for first time since 2002 amid spending-cut unrest**  
   Extending the sell-off in French government bonds, France's 10-year yield reached 5% for the first time since 2002, pushing borrowing costs to a 24-year high. The surge underscores investor concern as the government faces pressure to cut spending while deploying troops to contain violent protests demanding higher public outlays.

4. **IBM beats Q3 revenue and earnings estimates on AI demand**  
   IBM beat third-quarter top- and bottom-line expectations as CEO Arvind Krishna highlighted client demand in artificial intelligence.

5. **Jio's $3.2B IPO draws strong day-one demand despite Reliance and Nifty weakness**  
   Jio's $3.2 billion initial public offering saw overwhelming demand on its first day of bidding, contrasting with recent stock-market weakness in parent Reliance Industries and the broader Nifty index.
